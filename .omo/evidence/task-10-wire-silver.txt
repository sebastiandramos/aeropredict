## Funciones / colecciones / checkpoints creados

### src/aeropredict/opensky/storage_silver.py (ya presente en disco, verificado)
- Writers: `write_metar`, `write_holidays`, `write_eurocontrol_pru`, `write_notam`
- Collection getters con índices: `_get_metar_collection` ([icao_id, obs_time]),
  `_get_holidays_collection` ([date, name, source]),
  `_get_eurocontrol_collection` ([source_file, year]),
  `_get_notam_collection` ([snapshot_at, layer])
- Flags de índices: `_metar_indexes_ensure`, `_holidays_indexes_ensure`,
  `_eurocontrol_indexes_ensure`, `_notam_indexes_ensure`

### scripts/bronze_to_silver.py
- 5 doc builders puros: `_build_metar_docs`, `_build_nager_docs`,
  `_build_python_holidays_docs`, `_build_eurocontrol_docs`, `_build_notam_docs`
- Coercers numéricos: `_coerce_int`, `_coerce_float` (+ `_split_pipe`)
- Reader genérico `_read_bronze_table` + wrappers: `_read_bronze_metar`,
  `_read_bronze_holidays_nager`, `_read_bronze_holidays_python`,
  `_read_bronze_eurocontrol`, `_read_bronze_notam`
- Procesadores con checkpoint tras escritura correcta: `_process_metar_rows`
  (por hora UTC, patrón AENA), `_process_holidays_rows` (clave source_año),
  `_process_eurocontrol_rows` (clave filename_año), `_process_notam_rows`
  (clave snapshot_at)
- Colecciones checkpoint: `bronze_to_silver_metar`, `bronze_to_silver_holidays`,
  `bronze_to_silver_eurocontrol`, `bronze_to_silver_notam`
- main(): orden opensky → weather → aena → metar → holidays → eurocontrol → notam
- Docstring actualizado a 7 ingestiones; --dry-run conservado

### tests/
- tests/test_storage_silver_helpers.py: FakeCollection con insert_many +
  FakeBulkWriteError (subclase de pymongo BulkWriteError), 11 tests nuevos
  (success/empty-guard/bulk-error/índices de los 4 writers)
- tests/test_bronze_to_silver_collectors.py (NUEVO): 9 tests de los 5 builders
  puros con fixtures realistas (patrón importlib)
